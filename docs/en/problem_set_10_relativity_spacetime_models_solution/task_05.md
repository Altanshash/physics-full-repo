# Problem 5 — Muons in the Atmosphere

## Given

Muons are created at an altitude of:

<div align="center">

h = 10 km

</div>

<br>

Proper lifetime of a muon:

<div align="center">

τ₀ = 2.2 μs

</div>

<br>

Muon velocity:

<div align="center">

v = 0.995c

</div>

<br>

Speed of light:

<div align="center">

c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s

</div>

---

# Solution

## 1. Calculate the Lorentz Factor

Lorentz factor formula:

<div align="center">

γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²)

</div>

<br>

Since:

<div align="center">

v = 0.995c

</div>

<br>

we get:

<div align="center">

γ = 1 / √(1 − 0.995²)

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

γ = 1 / √(1 − 0.990025)

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

γ = 1 / √0.009975

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

γ ≈ 10.01

</div>

---

## 2. Mean Lifetime in Earth’s Frame

Time dilation formula:

<div align="center">

τ = γτ₀

</div>

<br>

Substitute the values:

<div align="center">

τ = 10.01 × 2.2 μs

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

τ ≈ 22.02 μs

</div>

<br>

So, in Earth’s frame, the muon lifetime is approximately:

<div align="center">

τ ≈ 22.02 μs

</div>

---

## 3. Average Distance Traveled by the Muon

Distance formula:

<div align="center">

d = vτ

</div>

<br>

Convert lifetime to seconds:

<div align="center">

τ = 22.02 × 10⁻⁶ s

</div>

<br>

Velocity:

<div align="center">

v = 0.995 × 3 × 10⁸

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

v = 2.985 × 10⁸ m/s

</div>

<br>

Now calculate distance:

<div align="center">

d = (2.985 × 10⁸)(22.02 × 10⁻⁶)

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

d ≈ 6573 m

</div>

<br>

Convert to kilometers:

<div align="center">

d ≈ 6.57 km

</div>

---

## 4. Without Relativity, Would the Muon Reach the Surface?

Without relativity, the muon lifetime would be:

<div align="center">

τ₀ = 2.2 μs

</div>

<br>

Distance without time dilation:

<div align="center">

d₀ = vτ₀

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

d₀ = (2.985 × 10⁸)(2.2 × 10⁻⁶)

</div>

<br>

<div align="center">

d₀ ≈ 657 m

</div>

<br>

Convert to kilometers:

<div align="center">

d₀ ≈ 0.657 km

</div>

<br>

Since:

<div align="center">

0.657 km < 10 km

</div>

<br>

without relativity, the muon would not reach the Earth’s surface.

---

# Conclusion

The Lorentz factor for a muon moving at:

<div align="center">

v = 0.995c

</div>

<br>

is approximately:

<div align="center">

γ ≈ 10.01

</div>

<br>

The muon lifetime in Earth’s frame becomes:

<div align="center">

τ ≈ 22.02 μs

</div>

<br>

The average distance traveled is:

<div align="center">

d ≈ 6.57 km

</div>

<br>

Without relativity, the muon would travel only:

<div align="center">

d₀ ≈ 0.657 km

</div>

<br>

Therefore, without relativistic time dilation, most muons would not reach the Earth’s surface from an altitude of 10 km.
