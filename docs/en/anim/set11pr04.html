The yellow walls represent an infinite potential well. The wave function must be zero at the walls: ψ(0) = 0 and ψ(L) = 0. For the ground state n = 1, the probability density is largest at the center of the well, x = L / 2. The energy levels increase as n² and decrease as 1/L².
