In the Bohr model, the electron can move only in certain allowed orbits. These orbits are called energy levels. When the electron jumps from a higher level to a lower level, it releases energy as a photon. In this animation, the electron moves from n = 3 to n = 2, producing red light with wavelength about 656 nm.
Higher orbit → higher energy. Lower orbit → lower energy. Difference is emitted as light.
