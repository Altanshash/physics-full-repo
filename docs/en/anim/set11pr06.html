The curve shows |ψ(x,t)|², the probability density of the wave packet. At first, the packet is narrow and localized. As time passes, it becomes wider and the peak becomes lower. This happens because the packet contains many momentum components. Different components move with different speeds, which illustrates the uncertainty principle.
