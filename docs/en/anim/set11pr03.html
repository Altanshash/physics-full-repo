Photoelectric Effect

Light hits a metal surface and emits electrons
Work function:
W = 3.2 eV

Threshold frequency:
f₀ = W / h

Kinetic energy:
Kmax = hf − W
Since f = 1.5f₀:

Photon energy is greater than the work function.

Kmax = 1.6 eV
Stopping potential = 1.6 V
LIGHT
metal surface
frequency: f = 1.5f₀ → electrons are emitted
Yellow particles represent photons. They strike the metal surface. If the light frequency is greater than the threshold frequency f₀, electrons are emitted. Increasing intensity sends more photons, so more electrons are emitted, but the maximum kinetic energy depends only on frequency, not intensity.
