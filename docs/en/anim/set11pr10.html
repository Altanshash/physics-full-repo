Special Relativity

Time dilation, length contraction, and constant speed of light
Speed of light:
c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s

Lorentz factor:
γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²)

Time dilation:
Δt = γΔt₀

Length contraction:
L = L₀ / γ
Example:
v = 0.8c

γ = 1 / √(1 − 0.8²)
γ ≈ 1.67

Moving clock runs slower.
Moving rocket appears shorter.

In special relativity, the speed of light is constant for all observers. When an object moves close to the speed of light, time dilation occurs: the moving clock runs slower. At the same time, the moving object appears shorter in the direction of motion. For v = 0.8c, the Lorentz factor is γ ≈ 1.67.
