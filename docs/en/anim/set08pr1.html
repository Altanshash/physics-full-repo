Problem Set 8 – Statistics and Measurements

Descriptive Statistics of a Measurement Series

Given

Measurements of a rod’s length in centimeters:

$$x = [12.10,\ 12.08,\ 12.12,\ 12.11,\ 12.09,\ 12.13,\ 12.07,\ 12.10]$$

Number of measurements:

$$n = 8$$

1. Arithmetic Mean

$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$$
$$\sum x_i = 96.80$$
$$\bar{x} = \frac{96.80}{8}$$
$$\bar{x} = 12.10\ \text{cm}$$

2. Sample Variance and Standard Deviation

$$s^2 = \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n - 1}$$
$$\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 = 0.0028$$
$$s^2 = \frac{0.0028}{7} = 0.0004\ \text{cm}^2$$
$$s = \sqrt{0.0004}$$
$$s = 0.02\ \text{cm}$$

3. Uncertainty of the Mean

$$u(\bar{x}) = \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$$
$$u(\bar{x}) = \frac{0.02}{\sqrt{8}}$$
$$u(\bar{x}) \approx 0.0071\ \text{cm}$$

4. Final Result

$$x = \bar{x} \pm u(\bar{x})$$
$$x = 12.10 \pm 0.0071$$
$$x = (12.10 \pm 0.007)\ \text{cm}$$

5. Confidence Intervals

$$\bar{x} \pm s = 12.10 \pm 0.02 = [12.08;\ 12.12]\ \text{cm}$$
$$\bar{x} \pm 2s = 12.10 \pm 0.04 = [12.06;\ 12.14]\ \text{cm}$$
$$\bar{x} \pm 3s = 12.10 \pm 0.06 = [12.04;\ 12.16]\ \text{cm}$$
68% – 95% – 99%

Conclusion

The average length of the rod is:

$$x = (12.10 \pm 0.007)\ \text{cm}$$

The standard deviation is:

$$s = 0.02\ \text{cm}$$

The uncertainty of the mean is:

$$u(\bar{x}) = 0.0071\ \text{cm}$$

The result is reliable and relatively precise.
