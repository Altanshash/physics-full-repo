Relativistic Length Contraction

Rocket Velocity: v = 0.6c

Tunnel
Rocket

Proper Length

120 m

Observed Length

96 m

Contraction

20%
Lorentz factor:

γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²)

γ = 1 / √(1 − 0.6²)

γ = 1.25

Length contraction:

L = L₀ / γ

L = 120 / 1.25 = 96 m
The moving rocket appears shorter to the stationary observer.
