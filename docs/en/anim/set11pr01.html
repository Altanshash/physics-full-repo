Wave-Particle Duality

de Broglie idea: an electron behaves like both a particle and a wave
ATOM
Electron wave: λ
Electron moves with velocity v
Atom size ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m
If velocity is doubled:
wavelength becomes shorter
The moving electron is shown as a blue particle. At the same time, it has a wave nature, represented by the green wave. The distance between two wave peaks is the de Broglie wavelength λ.

When the electron wavelength is close to the size of an atom, its wave behavior can be observed.
λ = h / mv    →    higher velocity means smaller wavelength
Therefore, when velocity increases, the wave effect becomes harder to observe.
