Quantum Tunneling

A particle tunnels through a potential barrier
Particle energy:
E = 3 eV

Barrier height:
U₀ = 5 eV

Barrier width:
a = 0.5 nm
Since: E < U₀

Classically: particle cannot cross the barrier.

Quantum mechanically: particle has a probability to tunnel through.

Example: Scanning Tunneling Microscope
E = 3 eV
U₀ = 5 eV
potential barrier
The blue particle approaches a potential barrier. Even though the particle energy E = 3 eV is smaller than the barrier height U₀ = 5 eV, quantum mechanics allows a probability that the particle can appear on the other side. This phenomenon is called quantum tunneling.
