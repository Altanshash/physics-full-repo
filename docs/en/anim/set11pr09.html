Nuclear Fission and Binding Energy

Mass defect is converted into released energy
Mass defect:
Δm = mnucleons − mnucleus

Energy released:
E = Δmc²

Given:
Δm = 0.2 u
Since 1 u = 931.5 MeV:

E = 0.2 × 931.5
E = 186.3 MeV

Nuclear energy is millions of times larger than chemical energy.

A heavy nucleus absorbs a neutron and splits into two smaller fragments. The missing mass, called mass defect, is converted into energy by E = Δmc². For Δm = 0.2 u, the released energy is 186.3 MeV. The binding energy per nucleon is greatest near iron, which is why nuclei around iron are the most stable.
